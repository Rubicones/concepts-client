# Usage note · Home

**Who, when, from where.** A client returning to start a campaign, or because an email said something is waiting. Desktop or phone, usually from a link.

**First thing to see.** Whether anything needs them. If a campaign is "Ready for your review", it leads the page as one navy card with one orange button ("Review directions"); more waiting campaigns are mentioned in a line, not stacked.

**Primary action.** "New campaign" (top right, where the eye ends). On the very first visit it becomes the hero's "Start a campaign", with the three steps (brief → directions to review → final) explained in one line each.

**Secondary.** Agencies: the client-brand picker next to the primary action, remembered across visits (cookie). Each campaign card opens the campaign.

**Cards.** Latest delivered keyframe (or a branded gradient with the kind's mark), name, kind and formats drawn to ratio, the client status pill, a quiet three-dot progress (current dot orange), "Updated …".

**What can go wrong.** Slow data → skeletons with the cards' exact shape. Questionnaire not approved → redirected to the questionnaire (it is the dashboard until then). Agency with one brand → no picker.

**How often.** Rarely; every visit should answer "what do I need to do now?" in one glance.
