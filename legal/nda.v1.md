This agreement is between Toggle Concepts (“we”) and the company named on the signing page (“you”). Both of us will share information to start working together. This agreement keeps it private.

## 1. What counts as confidential

Anything one side shares with the other: business data, plans, prices, results, brand materials, customer insights, briefs, drafts and finished work, in any form.

## 2. What we both promise

We use the other side's information only to work together. We share it only with people on our team who need it and are bound by the same duty. We protect it at least as carefully as our own.

## 3. What is not confidential

Information that is already public, that we already had, that we got lawfully from someone else, or that we created on our own without the other side's information.

## 4. Your work and your clients

[[agency]]We never contact your clients and never say we worked for them without your written OK. The work goes out under your name.[[/agency]][[brand]]We don't show your work or name you as a client without your written OK.[[/brand]]

## 5. How long it lasts

3 years after our work together ends. On request, each side deletes or returns the other side's materials.
